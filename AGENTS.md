# Working on Grdn

For landing-page scenes or animation, read `MOTION.md` and the relevant scene script before editing. `MOTION.md` captures the owner's approved behaviour and minimum review standard; it supersedes older motion directions in `HANDOFF.md` and initial scene drafts.

- Reuse Pointy's shared character, companion placement, handoff and playback behaviour. Do not invent a different version for each scene.
- Design and build one scene at a time. Write its trigger, beats, result, interruption and resume behaviour first. Do not add visitor interactions to observational scenes unless requested.
- Treat quality as part of implementation. Review motion in the browser, refine it, and report what was actually checked. A screenshot or passing syntax check alone does not verify animation.
- Preserve the approved opening copy, wandering canvas and existing visual language unless the task changes them.
- Keep work local unless publication is requested. Pushing this repository can deploy the website.

- Commit each new experience separately, including its scene script and related implementation changes. Keep future experience work out of another experience’s commit.

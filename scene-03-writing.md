# Scene 03 — A story taking shape

Wen, a reporter, writes a short Fred Rogers profile in a note within Grdn. As the note develops, the adjacent Stream becomes more specific. There is no Ask request, search field, or visitor interaction. The scene depicts the owner's intended contextual Stream behaviour; the current browser extension does not implement live keystroke reading. Keep the depiction inside Grdn rather than implying that the extension watches another document.

The note uses original paraphrases, not attributed quotations:

1. “Fred Rogers made television for children.”
2. “Music was part of how he helped children understand their feelings.”
3. “In 1969, he took that same care to a Senate hearing on public television.”

The Stream first offers a broad “About Fred” background reference from `fredrogersinstitute.org`. Once Wen writes about music, “The Music” from `misterrogers.org` appears with a short reason about songs and feelings. Once Wen writes the 1969 detail, “About Fred Rogers” from `misterrogers.org` appears with the reason “1969 Senate testimony.” These are real page titles/domains, with original concise reasons; no fabricated paper, author, or source quote. Broad context remains visible as more specific references arrive.

The scene replaces Type & letters with a 580px desktop / 400px narrow stage. Pointy joins Wen's cursor, dwells while Wen types, guides attention to the music and testimony results after each paragraph, then rejoins Wen using shared handoffs. The stream's changes follow completed writing beats rather than racing the text. One pausable clock drives text, cards, emphasis, and actors. Above 60% stage visibility and after settling, Pointy joins; below 50%, when the intro reopens, or when the document hides, content freezes while Pointy returns to the visitor. Re-entry rejoins the frozen pose before playback resumes. The active frame follows ownership. Reduced motion shows the completed note and three source cards without flights or typewriter effects.

After Wander, all three scenes have directional edge markers available together. Writing can be chosen before either other scene finishes. Clicking its marker pans there at the fixed device scale; manual navigation cancels the pan. Hide its marker at 60% stage visibility and re-show it below 50%, regardless of playback state. Once settled, Pointy gives one gentle attention nudge before joining Wen. A deliberate finished revisit gets a brief acknowledgment of the result, without replaying the note or Stream. Completed scenes remain linkable from offscreen. Pointy's name stays out of visitor-facing copy and accessibility labels.

## Source evidence and product scope

The [Fred Rogers Institute's “About Fred”](https://www.fredrogersinstitute.org/about-fred) confirms his work in children's television, music studies, child-development work, and 1969 testimony. The official [Mister Rogers' Neighborhood “The Music”](https://misterrogers.org/the-music/) describes his music composition degree and songs as a way to make child-development ideas understandable to children. Its [“About Fred Rogers”](https://misterrogers.org/about-fred-rogers/) describes the 1969 Senate public-television funding testimony. The Stream here is an observational prototype of the owner's requested contextual behaviour.
